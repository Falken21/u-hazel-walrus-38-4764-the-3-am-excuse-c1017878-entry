A one-line comedy entry written in the voice of a hardware wallet that
refuses to sign a transaction at 3 a.m. The wallet is not malfunctioning;
it is the only sober party in the room. It reads its owner's condition
like a status screen -- eyes at 4%, judgement in airplane mode -- and
delivers the one argument a tired human cannot refute: the funds are
patient, but regret delivers instantly. Part bouncer, part battery
indicator, it signs nothing until morning.

## The entry

> Signing declined. It is 3 a.m., your eyes are at 4% and your judgement is in airplane mode. The funds will still be here at nine; your regret would arrive instantly.

165 characters.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
